Key sections

Background & aim

Summarises history-dependent behaviour (HDB) and asks how prior exposure may affect a later microbial response.

Mechanisms

Chromatin state, protein inheritance and metabolic state are discussed in the reviewed literature.

Case study

The poster describes yeast responses during shifts between carbon sources, including changes in growth lag.

Data & limits

The displayed tallies are descriptive. The source is a narrative review, not a pooled meta-analysis. Direct links to environmental pollution remain an open question.

Literature review & reference

Vermeersch, L., Cool, L., Gorkovskiy, A., Voordeckers, K., Wenseleers, T., & Verstrepen, K. J. (2022). Do microbes have a memory? History-dependent behavior in the adaptation to variable environments. Frontiers in Microbiology, 13, 1004488.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2022.1004488
